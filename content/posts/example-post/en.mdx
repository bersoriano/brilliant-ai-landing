This post is a template. Copy the `example-post` folder, rename it to your slug, and set `draft: false` when the post is ready. The page already shows the title and description from the frontmatter, so start the body with a paragraph, not a `#` heading.

## Section headings use two hashes

Paragraphs are plain text. Use **bold** for the one phrase that matters and *italics* sparingly. Links to site pages follow the post's language: [how we handle data](/security) goes to `/es/security` in the Spanish version.

### Lists

- Name the workflow and who approves it.
- Say what stays with a person.
- Keep numbers honest: label illustrative figures as examples.

1. Ordered steps work too.
2. Keep them short.

> A pull quote renders in the display face.

| Step | Owner | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Intake | Assistant | Draft record |
| Review | Your team | Approved record |

Images go in `public/blog/<slug>/` and are referenced as `/blog/<slug>/image.jpg`. Remote images are rejected by the tests.
